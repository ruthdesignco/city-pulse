## Where the board agrees
- The stack fits. Vite + r3f is right for a canvas-first SPA, and Supabase cron is the cheapest scheduler that can hit sub-2-minute cadence.
- Keep the 511 key and service role key in Edge Function secrets only. Only the anon key gets a `VITE_` prefix.
- Move freshness into Weekend 1. Interpolation keeps stale buses gliding, which hides a dead feed. Dim or stop buses when data is more than ~3 min old.
- Muni: a short cron tick (15–30s) that calls 511 only when a DB row says ≥75s have passed and the hourly budget allows it.
- Push model: most of the board (Hightower, Kleppmann, Quinn, plus Hunt's Broadcast concern) prefers the client fetching a single "current state" view or snapshot every ~20–60s over per-row Realtime. It's simpler, reconnect-safe, cost doesn't grow per row per viewer, and there's no publish surface.
- Privacy is enforced at write time. Whatever reaches `incidents` is readable with the anon key.
